reference is truthful, is no more prominent than needed, and does not suggest sponsorship or
endorsement:

  "Built with Stance", "Compatible with Stance", "A Stance theme for Figma".
- Refer to Stance by name in articles, tutorials, talks, reviews, courses and academic work.

You are free to fork Stance and to distribute modified versions under the License. When you do:

- give the fork a different name and remove the Stance and Binds logos from it;

- keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files, and state in your NOTICE that you changed the files, as
