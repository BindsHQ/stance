You may make accurate, **nominative** references to Stance and Binds, provided that the
reference is truthful, is no more prominent than needed, and does not suggest sponsorship or
endorsement:

- State that your product is built with, uses, or is compatible with Stance. For example:
  "Built with Stance", "Compatible with Stance", "A Stance theme for Figma".
- Refer to Stance by name in articles, tutorials, talks, reviews, courses and academic work.
- Keep the copyright, license, notice and attribution statements that ship with the software,
  as Section 4 of the License requires.
- Name the official packages, for example `@bindshq/stance-react`, in your dependency lists,

Use the plain word mark in text. Do not use the logo for these references.

## 4. The Binds brand colours

Ivory `#FCF6EB` and Indigo `#140C2E` are the identity of Binds. They are the default palette in
Stance because Stance is the design system Binds products are built with.

- **Your product uses your colours.** Any product, service, website or app built with Stance
  that is made available to anyone outside your own development team must replace the Binds
  colour pair with its own brand colours before release. Stance is built to be themed: see
  [Theming](https://www.stanceui.com/docs/theming).
- **You may use the default palette** while developing and evaluating Stance, in tests, and in
  articles, tutorials and screenshots that are about Stance itself.
- **Do not imitate it.** Do not use the Ivory and Indigo pair, or colours chosen to be
  indistinguishable from it, as the identity of another product, company or service.

The token values remain software under the License. This section governs their use as brand
identity, which the License does not grant.

You are free to fork Stance and to distribute modified versions under the License. When you do:

- give the fork a different name and remove the Stance and Binds logos from it;
- you may say "based on Stance" in its description, in plain text;
- keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files, and state in your NOTICE that you changed the files, as
  Section 4 of the License requires;

title "Trademark request". Describe the use, where it will appear and for how long. Permission
